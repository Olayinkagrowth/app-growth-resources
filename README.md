# App Growth Resources

Practical mobile app marketing resources by NEXATOP, focused on app store optimization (ASO), user acquisition, and sustainable app growth.

## About This Repository

This repository brings together practical frameworks and checklists to help mobile app founders improve app discoverability, strengthen store listings, and plan user acquisition more effectively.

## What You'll Find Here

- **App Store Optimization (ASO):** Keyword research, store listing improvements, and conversion optimization.
- **User Acquisition:** Paid and organic acquisition planning for iOS and Android apps.
- **App Launch Planning:** Checklists for launch preparation, early visibility, and first-user acquisition.
- **Growth Audits:** Frameworks for reviewing app positioning, target audiences, acquisition channels, and growth opportunities.
- **Marketing Resources:** Practical templates and guidance for app founders and indie developers.

## Our Approach

Effective app growth goes beyond downloads. It involves reaching the right audience, communicating clear value, improving the store-page experience, and measuring meaningful user actions.
